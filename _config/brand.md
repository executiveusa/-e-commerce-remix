# TECITO DE LA VERDAD — Brand System

## Identity
- Primary mark: TECITO DE LA VERDAD
- Tagline ES: Encuentra tu verdad
- Tagline EN: Find your truth
- Primary language: Spanish
- Secondary language: English via explicit toggle

## Palette
- Obsidiana: `#1a1a1a`
- Cantera: `#f5efe6`
- Copal: `#c96f4a`
- Oro Viejo: `#b8954a`
- Barro: `#8b6f5c`
- Nopal: `#4a6741`

Usage: Obsidiana + Cantera carry most of the visual weight. Copal is action. Oro Viejo is reserved for luxury accents. Nopal is sparse.

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display class editorial serif.
- Body/UI: refined neutral sans-serif.
- Quote treatment: italic serif.

## Voice
Warm, direct, bilingual, ritualistic, never preachy. Avoid wellness jargon, toxic positivity, stock luxury language, generic AI copy, and fake exclusivity.

## Art direction
Mexico City as a lived place: golden-hour terraces, real ceramic and linen textures, warm amber grade, natural light, subtle human presence, tea always visible.
